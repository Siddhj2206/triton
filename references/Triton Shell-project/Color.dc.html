How roles are used

  • Primary is action and focus. One filled primary per view.
  • Tertiary is warmth: file types, unread dots, things to notice. Never an action.
  • Containers carry state: toggled on, selected, active workspace.

States and signals

  • Hover · focus · press · drag8 · 10 · 10 · 16 %
  • Disabled content · container38 · 12 %
  • Signal LEDs. 8px dots only, never fills.
